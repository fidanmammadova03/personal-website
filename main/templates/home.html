About Me

I’m Fidan, a Data & Business Analyst focused on e-commerce analytics, pricing, and product performance.

My work is about understanding what’s happening in the business and making it easier to act on. I use data to connect operations, pricing, stock, and performance so decisions are clear and practical.

Since December 2023, I’ve been working with VIGO Industries, supporting analytics across partners like Amazon, Wayfair, Lowe’s, and Home Depot. I analyze sales, pricing, stock, and performance data to identify issues and opportunities, and help structure that into clear reporting.

I build dashboards, automate repetitive data tasks, and work with different data sources to keep reporting consistent.

I have a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Khazar University and I’m currently pursuing a Master’s in Intelligent Systems. I’m interested in applying AI to business problems, especially in areas like pricing and decision-making.

I mainly work with Python and SQL, and build analytical workflows with Power BI, Tableau, Streamlit, and modern data libraries.
